Topics

Politics

On average, 34% of the short videos analyzed covered political topics. We found that the platform on which the video was posted had little effect on the share of posts about politics. However, we observed that digital outlets had a significantly smaller share of posts covering politics compared to public broadcast outlets, with a difference of -0.17. Finally, the share of posts about politics varied across countries, ranging from 18% in Croatia to 59% in Poland.

Law enforcement and crime

Our analysis of short videos revealed that, on average, 31% of posts covered law enforcement and crime. We found small, non-significant differences between TikTok (31%) and YouTube Shorts (33%). However, we observed that digital outlets had a significantly lower share of posts on this topic compared to private (8% higher) and public broadcast (10% higher) outlets. We observed a considerable range across countries, with Peru (50%) and Mexico (45%) having the highest shares, while Italy (19%) and Spain (20%) exhibited the lowest.

  • by outlet type
  • by country

War disasters accidents

We found that, on average, 21% of short video posts covered war, disasters, and accidents. We observed a significant, albeit small, difference between platforms, with YouTube Shorts (24%) featuring slightly more of this content than TikTok (22%). More substantial differences emerged across outlet types: digital outlets had the lowest share (10%), with print (18%) and private broadcast outlets (18%) showing higher shares. Public broadcast outlets posted the most content about war, disasters, and accidents, with a share of 34%. Across countries, we saw variance in the prevalence of this content, ranging from 13% in Indonesia, Peru, and Slovakia to 33% in South Africa.

  • by outlet type
  • by country

Economy and finance

Our analysis of short video posts revealed that, on average, 21% of the content focused on economy and finance. We found minimal differences between TikTok (22%) and YouTube Shorts (23%). Similarly, the type of outlet (digital, print, private broadcast, public broadcast) showed small and non-significant differences in their coverage of these topics. However, there were notable variations across countries; for example, Turkey and Brazil showed notably lower shares (14%), while Taiwan showed the highest share (31%).

  • by country

Social affairs

On average, 17% of the analyzed short video posts covered social affairs. We observed significant differences across platforms: TikTok showed a higher share of social affairs content compared to YouTube Shorts (a difference of 7%). Furthermore, public broadcast outlets featured a significantly higher proportion of social affairs posts compared to digital outlets, with an 8% difference. Finally, there was some variation across countries, with Kenya and South Africa showing the highest share (24%) and Hong Kong the lowest (11%).

Health and fitness

We found that, on average, 11% of short video posts covered health and fitness topics. We found no significant difference between TikTok and Youtube Shorts platforms. However, we observed a notable contrast between outlet types: public broadcast outlets had a higher share of health and fitness content (13%) compared to print outlets (8%). Across countries, Brazil showed a higher share of health and fitness content (16%) while Malaysia and Italy were among the lowest (8%).

Mobility and infrastructure

Our analysis of short videos revealed that mobility and infrastructure were covered in an average of 9% of posts. We found a significant difference between platforms: YouTube Shorts had a 3% higher share of posts covering these topics compared to TikTok. There were also small, but significant differences across outlet types with print media having a higher share of posts (12%) compared to digital media (8%), private broadcasts (9%) and public broadcasts (9%). Country-level analysis showed variation, with Thailand having the highest share at 16% and Romania and Spain the lowest at 7%.

Environment and energy

We found that, on average, 7% of short video posts covered environment and energy topics. There were no significant differences between TikTok and YouTube Shorts. However, we observed significant contrasts across different outlet types: public broadcast outlets had a higher share of posts covering these topics compared to digital outlets, with a difference of 7 percentage points. Print and private broadcast outlets also showed a higher share compared to digital outlets, with differences of 4 and 3 percentage points respectively. Across countries, the share of posts varied, with Australia showing a notably higher share at 11%.

Science technology and education

We found that, on average, 12% of short video posts across platforms covered science, technology, and education. While the platform (TikTok vs Youtube Shorts) did not significantly impact this share, we observed differences based on the outlet type, with public broadcast outlets showing a significantly higher share (18%) of science-related content compared to digital outlets (9%). There were also some differences across countries, but these were small.

Culture and arts

We found that, on average, 14% of short video posts across platforms covered culture and arts. There was little difference in the share of culture and arts content between TikTok (13%) and YouTube Shorts (12%). However, we observed significant differences based on the type of outlet, with print media showing a higher share of culture and arts content (17%) compared to digital outlets (12%), private broadcast (11%), and public broadcast (12%). At the country level, the share varied considerably, ranging from 9% in Brazil, South Korea and the United States to 23% in Italy and Spain.

The differences between outlet types were small and non-significant. At the country level, Switzerland had the highest share of posts covering lifestyle and travel (30%), while other countries varied from 5% (Hong Kong, South Korea, Taiwan, Peru) to 14% (Germany).

Across countries, we noticed some variation, with Switzerland showing the highest share of food-related content (16%), while Indonesia and Romania also had notably high shares at 12% each.

Pets and animals

There were only small and non-significant differences between the platforms TikTok and YouTube Shorts. However, we observed a significant contrast in outlet types, with digital outlets showing a 3 percentage point higher share of pet and animal content compared to public broadcast outlets. Across different countries, the share of posts about pets and animals varied, but these differences were not statistically significant.

Entertainment news and industry

However, we found significant contrasts in the share of entertainment content by outlet type: public broadcast outlets (18%) posted significantly more entertainment news than digital outlets (9%), and private broadcast outlets (14%) also posted significantly more entertainment news than digital outlets. Across countries, we saw some variation, ranging from Brazil and Malaysia at around 6-7% to Croatia at 24%, with other countries like Belgium (21%) also having high shares.

At the country level, Spain and Argentina had the highest shares, with 23% and 22%, respectively, while Indonesia showed the lowest share at 10%.

Across countries, we saw considerable variation, with Hungary having the highest share at 17%, while Nigeria and South Africa showed the lowest, around 1%.
