Publication patterns

The Platform News Report is based on data from 571 news outlets across 46 countries. We collected 185,969 posts published by these outlets on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts from March 15 to April 15, 2025.

Platform Outlets Posts %
TikTok 472 85,696 46
Instagram 486 62,936 34
YouTube Shorts 395 37,337 20

While Instagram had the biggest market share in terms of active news outlets, the amount of content posted on TikTok was much larger overall, with almost half of all posts in our sample coming from this platform, followed by Instagram, and YouTube Shorts, which also attracted far fewer outlets overall.

Frequency

News outlets, on average, published around 4.2 short video posts per day. Posting frequency varied significantly by platform, with outlets posting most frequently on TikTok, averaging 5.5 posts daily, followed by Instagram with 4.0 posts, and YouTube Shorts with 2.9 posts per day. Outlet type also played a significant role; private broadcasters were the most prolific, publishing 6.2 posts daily, considerably more than digital outlets, which averaged 3.5 posts. Public broadcasters averaged 4.2 posts per day, also more than digital outlets. Print outlets exhibited the lowest frequency, with an average of 2.8 posts daily. Posting frequencies also varied widely by country, ranging from less than 1 post per day in Denmark to almost 10 posts per day in Malaysia.

Video duration

The average duration of news video posts across all platforms and outlets was approximately 80 seconds. Examining platform differences, videos on TikTok averaged 84 seconds and Instagram videos averaged 82 seconds, both of comparable length. YouTube Shorts videos, however, were notably shorter, averaging 67 seconds. For outlet types, private broadcasters posted significantly longer videos, averaging 87 seconds, compared to digital outlets, which averaged 76 seconds. Print outlets also published videos that were significantly longer than those from digital outlets, by an average of 8 seconds. Country-specific trends revealed substantial variation, with videos ranging from an average of 52 seconds in Slovakia to 143 seconds in Thailand, highlighting a wide global spectrum in content length strategies.

Content duplication

News outlets globally cross-posted an average of 17% of their short video content across different platforms. We observed notable differences in duplication rates depending on the platform and outlet type. YouTube Shorts had a higher share of duplicated posts, at 21%, compared to TikTok and Instagram, both at 16%. Public broadcasters showed the highest duplication rates among outlet types, with 26% of their posts being duplicates, substantially more than digital outlets (12%), print outlets (16%), and private broadcasters (16%). Across countries, duplication varied widely, ranging from a mere 2% in places like Greece, Morocco, and Taiwan, to over half of all posts in countries such as Australia and Ireland (59%), Canada (55%), and the United Kingdom (50%).

Measures

Video duration was provided as meta data from the platforms. Content duplication was measured by computing pairwise Jaccard distances on the post transcripts, with a threshold of .70, using the textreuse package (Mullen 2020).
